## What's New
History. Open History from More to see every step by name, like Add Button or Move Card. Tap one to go back to it. The steps after it wait, dimmed, in case you change your mind.

Snapshots. Take a snapshot to keep a named copy of the canvas, and restore it whenever you like. Snapshots save with your project, on every device.

Blend modes. Pick a part and choose how it meets what is under it: join the lines, draw over, fill only the empty cells, or knock out its whole box.

A cleaner nav bar, with Settings and your Profile one tap away.
