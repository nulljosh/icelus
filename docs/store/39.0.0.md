## What's New
Blend modes. Pick a part and choose how it meets what is under it. Join lets box lines merge where they cross, Over draws on top without joining, Under only fills the empty cells, and Knock out clears its whole box. Change it any time.

A cleaner nav bar. Settings and your Profile each have their own button now, and each opens one simple sheet. Sign in, sign out or delete your account from Profile.

Buttons, fields and menus on your screens stand a little taller, so they read like the real thing.
