## What's New
Heal. Delete a box in the middle of a flow and the arrows on either side join up, still pinned at both ends.

Expand. Add a row to a table or an item to a list and it carries the pattern on: Item 3 then Item 4, Mon then Tue, January then February.

Actions. Open History, tap Record Action, make a few edits, then stop. Select any other part or screen and tap Play to make the same edits there.

History. Every step listed by name. Tap one to go back to it. Snapshots keep named copies of your canvas.

Blend modes, and a cleaner nav bar with Settings and your Profile one tap away.
