# App Store text for 46.0.0

Ships after 38.0.0 clears review (one build in review at a time; 39.0.0 to 46.0.0 roll into one build).

## Promotional text (170 max)
Symbol copies that keep their own words or style, and swap in place. Sizes, auto layout and constraints. Heal, harmonize, actions, History.

## What's New
Properties. Select a copy of a symbol and turn on Own words or Own style. Edit the other copies and this one keeps what it owns. Swap puts another symbol in its place, right where it sits.

Sizes. Select a screen and tap Make sizes: phone, tablet and desktop widths side by side. Change the words or color in one and the others follow.

Auto layout. Select a screen and turn on Auto layout. Its parts stay stacked with an even gap. Make some text longer and everything below moves down.

Constraints. Pin a part to the right, the bottom, the center or both sides of its screen. Resize the screen and it follows.

Harmonize, heal and expand. A dropped part takes on the look of its screen. Delete a box in a flow and its arrows join up. Lists and tables carry their pattern on.

Actions and History. Record a few edits and play them on any part. Every step listed by name, tap one to go back.
