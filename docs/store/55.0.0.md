# App Store text for 55.0.0

Ships once 50.0.0 clears review (one build in review at a time).

## Promotional text (170 max)
Link a button to a screen, then Play: boxes tick, tabs switch and fields take typing, like a real app. Inspect any part: its size, the gap on each side and its own code.

## What's New
Live parts. In Play, checkboxes tick, switches flip, segments and tabs switch, stars rate and fields take typing. Your design stays as it was.

Play mode. Under Export, Play shows one screen at a time. Tap a linked button to open its screen, Back to go back, Done to stop.

Hotspots. Pick a button, open More and choose the screen it opens. A small ring marks it, and a dashed line points to its screen while it is picked. Copy all screens and the build brief list each link by the words on the button.

Copy. Every word on the canvas in one list under More. Edit any of it there and only those words change.

Access check. Review now flags controls that focus would visit out of reading order, fields with no label, and controls too small to tap. Fix Focus Order sorts them in one step.

Inspect. Pick a part and open More: where it sits, its size, the gap to its neighbours on each side, and its own React or SwiftUI code to copy.

Grids. Select a screen and set Columns and Gutter. A part you drag snaps onto a column.

Libraries. Make parts into symbols and copy them as a library. Paste a newer version and every linked copy updates, except what a copy owns.

Variables and modes. Draw every part in ASCII, single, heavy, double or rounded lines at once. Bind a screen's gap or padding to a named spacing value.
