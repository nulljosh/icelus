## Promotional text (170 max)
Auto layout and constraints: screens that space themselves, grow with your text and resize cleanly. Heal, harmonize, actions, History.

## What's New
Auto layout. Select a screen and turn on Auto layout. Its parts stay stacked with an even gap. Make some text longer and everything below moves down. Delete a part and the rest closes up. Drag one to reorder.

Constraints. Pin a part to the right, the bottom, the center or both sides of its screen. Resize the screen and it follows.

Harmonize. A part you drop on a screen takes on the look the other parts share.

Heal and expand. Delete a box in a flow and its arrows join up. Lists and tables carry their pattern on.

Find distractions. Review spots stray marks and empty parts and clears them in one tap.

Actions and History. Record a few edits and play them on any part. Every step listed by name, tap one to go back.
