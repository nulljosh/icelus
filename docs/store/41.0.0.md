## What's New
Actions. Open History, tap Record Action, and make a few edits: nudge a part, make it bold, change its color. Open History again and stop. Now select any other part or screen and tap Play to make the same edits there. Actions save with your project.

History. Every step listed by name, like Add Button or Move Card. Tap one to go back to it. The steps after it wait, dimmed, in case you change your mind.

Snapshots. Keep a named copy of the canvas and restore it whenever you like.

Blend modes. Choose how a part meets what is under it: join the lines, draw over, fill only the empty cells, or knock out its whole box.

A cleaner nav bar, with Settings and your Profile one tap away.
