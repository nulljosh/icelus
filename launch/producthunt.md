## First comment

Hi, I'm Josh, I built Icelus.

Every wireframe tool exports an image nobody can edit in a pull request or a chat. Icelus draws the wireframe out of characters. Pick a component, place it on the grid, copy. It pastes into a README, an issue, a commit message, a Slack thread, and stays editable everywhere. Listed on the App Store as Wiretext.

$0.99 on the App Store, free on web. Happy to answer anything.
