Name: Icelus

One-liner: Sketch an interface out of plain characters.

Description: A wireframe tool that draws interfaces in Unicode instead of images. Pick a component, stamp it on a monospace grid, copy the result as text that pastes into a commit message, an issue, or a chat. $0.99 on the App Store, free on web.
