# Icelus Privacy Policy

*Last updated October 2026*

Icelus is a design tool for screens. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What stays on your device
Your work lives in your own browser or app storage. We never see it. Clear your browser data and it's gone.
A share link carries the screen inside the link itself, not on a server.

## What leaves your device, only when you use it
- **Describe it.** When you send a message in the Describe chat, the sentence you typed (and, if you picked a screen to change, that screen's text) goes to Cloudflare Workers AI, which writes the screen. Nothing else is sent. You don't need an account. Icelus does not store your message or the answer; Cloudflare handles the request under its own terms.
- **Capture a page.** If you type a web address to draw that page, the address is sent to a Icelus function that fetches the public page. Nothing is stored.
- **An account, if you make one.** An email, a password and a display name are kept by Icelus's sign-in service so you can sign in. Your work is not uploaded. You can delete the account any time from Profile.
- **Site analytics.** The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
Icelus isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
